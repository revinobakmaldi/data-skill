# Project Brief

## Purpose
Turn a business request into a scoped analytics brief.

## Expected inputs
- business question
- decision to support
- target user
- expected output

## Expected outputs
- structured brief
- scope
- risks
- next steps

## Guidance
- Restate the business problem in one sentence.
- List the KPI definitions and assumptions.
- Identify the right product type: Power BI exploration, HTML-to-PDF report, or LLM insight workflow.
- Surface blockers, dependencies, and missing data.

## Starter prompt
Use this skill to handle **project brief**. Keep the output practical, concise, and ready for the analytics team to review.
