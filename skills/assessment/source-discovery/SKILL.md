# Source Discovery

## Purpose
Map likely sources, joins, grain, and data risks before build.

## Expected inputs
- business question
- known systems
- warehouse access

## Expected outputs
- source map
- grain notes
- join notes
- risk list

## Guidance
- Identify candidate tables, files, or APIs.
- State likely business keys and join paths.
- Flag freshness, ownership, and missing-field risk.
- Keep output concise and decision-oriented.

## Starter prompt
Use this skill to handle **source discovery**. Keep the output practical, concise, and ready for the analytics team to review.
