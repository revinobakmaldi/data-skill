What should the team actually use?

This is the decision view, not another list. It maps real external resources to workflow stage, likely owner, adoption recommendation, and why each one matters. Use it to separate clone-now tools from reference-only noise.

Adopt now

  • Use when the tool matches your actual stack today, Airflow, dbt, Power BI, HTML/PDF reporting, data-to-LLM analysis.
  • These are the first resources worth operationalizing, not just bookmarking.

Evaluate

  • Strong candidate, but team fit, implementation cost, or overlap still needs testing.
  • Good for pilots before standardization.

Reference only

  • Useful patterns, examples, or niche capability, but not worth making core right now.
  • Keep for inspiration, not immediate rollout.
